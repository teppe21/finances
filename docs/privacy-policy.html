Privacy Policy

Application: Finances (com.teppe21.finances)
Developer: teppe21
Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Guaranteed Air-Gap: Finances does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal, financial, or behavioral telemetry. All data is processed and stored 100% locally on your Android device.

1. Overview and Core Philosophy

Finances is a native Android personal finance manager designed from the ground up to respect your financial privacy. Unlike conventional cloud-based personal financial apps that upload bank statements, transaction histories, and identity credentials to third-party remote servers, Finances operates on a local-first architecture.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, process, or transmit any of the following:

  • No Analytics or Telemetry: There are no third-party tracking SDKs (no Google Firebase Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Facebook SDK, etc.).
  • No Account Registration: You do not need to create an account, log in with an email address, or provide phone numbers.
  • No Cloud Synchronisation: Your transactions, accounts, receipts, and budgets remain exclusively in your phone's local SQLite database.
  • No Advertising: The application contains no ads and no advertising identifiers.

3. Android Notification Access (NotificationListenerService)

Finances offers an optional feature that allows you to automatically record transactions when your banking apps post payment notifications.

  • Supported Banks: Only notifications from explicit, whitelisted banking and payment applications (OTP Bank, Revolut, Google Wallet, Erste Bank, MBH Bank, and Wise) are handled.
  • Zero Raw Text Persistence: Raw notification titles, notification text, and notification summaries are never stored in the database. Only sanitized, structured transaction metadata (such as amount, currency, timestamp, and parsed merchant) is recorded locally.
  • Automatic 2FA / OTP Discard: Security codes, one-time passwords, login tokens, and verification codes are discarded immediately before processing.
  • Marketing & Referral Filtering: Non-transactional alerts (such as referral promotions, friend invites, or marketing bonuses) are automatically detected and discarded.
  • Complete User Control: Notification access requires your explicit permission in Android system settings and can be revoked at any time.

4. Camera and Receipt OCR Processing

The receipt scanning feature uses your device's camera to capture photos of paper receipts:

  • 100% On-Device OCR: Optical Character Recognition is performed entirely on your device using Google ML Kit On-Device Text Recognition.
  • No Cloud Image Uploads: Captured receipt images and extracted text snippets never leave your device.

5. Multi-Currency and Foreign Exchange (FX) Rates

Finances includes real-time multi-currency support and currency conversion:

  • Public Rate Queries: If enabled, the application queries public European Central Bank reference exchange rates (via the open-source frankfurter.app API). These HTTP GET queries request standard market currency pairs (e.g., EUR/HUF) and transmit zero personal, account, or financial data.
  • Air-Gapped Offline Mode: You can completely disable automatic FX sync in Settings. In offline mode, the app uses cached rates or custom user-defined manual exchange rates without sending a single network byte.

6. Device Security & App Lock

You can secure access to the app using an on-device 4-digit PIN code or Android Biometric authentication (fingerprint / face unlock):

  • Salted Cryptographic Hashing: PIN codes are stored using salted SHA-256 hashes generated with a cryptographically random salt. Raw PINs are never stored in plaintext.
  • Biometric Security: Biometric authentication is handled by the official AndroidX BiometricPrompt API; biometric credentials never leave the Android Secure Hardware Enclave.

7. Data Retention, Export & Complete Deletion

You retain absolute ownership of your data at all times:

  • Local CSV Export: You can export your transaction records to a standard CSV file whenever you choose.
  • Complete Data Wipe: The "Delete All Financial Data" action in Settings permanently wipes all transactions, accounts, rules, receipts, and notification history from SQLite and resets your preferences.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application immediately deletes all local databases, preferences, and cached receipts stored in the application's private sandboxed directory.
